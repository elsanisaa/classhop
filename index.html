    What ClassHop does

    HKS students can cross-register at other Harvard schools, MIT and Tufts Fletcher, but each school lists classes in its own catalog and on its own time grid. ClassHop puts your classes on one week, works out the travel time for every move between campuses, and flags moves you can't make, tight connections and days without a lunch break, before the cross-registration deadline.

    Privacy: no account, no tracking. Your week is saved only in this browser. "Copy my link" packs your week into a web link so you can open it on another device; anyone you send that link to can see your class times.

    How the times are worked out

    Spare time = the gap between one class ending and the next starting, minus the travel time. Walking is estimated from the distance between buildings × 1.3 for street routes at 4.8 km/h; cycling at 15 km/h plus 3 minutes to unlock and lock. "T + walk" = walk to the station, average wait, ride, walk from the station. "MBTA bus" = walk to the stop, half the usual gap between buses, the ride, and the walk from the stop. Bus times are estimates until the MBTA timetable loads. For live Harvard shuttle tracking, open a trip in Citymapper. The first class of each day is not checked, because the tool doesn't know where you start.
