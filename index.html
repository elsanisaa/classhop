    How the times are worked out

    Spare time = the gap between one class ending and the next starting, minus the travel time. Walking is estimated from the distance between buildings × 1.3 for street routes at 4.8 km/h; cycling at 15 km/h plus 3 minutes to unlock and lock. "T + walk" = walk to the station, average wait, ride, walk from the station. Between Harvard buildings the tool uses walking or cycling; for Harvard shuttles, open the trip in Citymapper. The first class of each day is not checked, because the tool doesn't know where you start.
